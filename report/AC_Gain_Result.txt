  TWO-STAGE CMOS OTA: FINAL AC ANALYSIS REPORT

1. OPEN-LOOP DC GAIN (Av)
   - Value: ~80 dB
   - Details: Measured from the open-loop differential frequency response,
     providing robust low-frequency voltage amplification.

2. UNITY-GAIN BANDWIDTH (UGBW)
   - Value: ~5.23 MHz to 5.59 MHz
   - Details: Verified via open-loop crossover (~5.23 MHz) and confirmed
     by the closed-loop -3 dB cutoff frequency (5.587 MHz at -3.02 dB).

3. PHASE MARGIN (PM)
   - Value: ~86.7°
   - Details: Confirmed by open-loop phase at the 0 dB crossing and the 
     smooth, peak-free closed-loop phase rollout (-48.69° at the 
     bandwidth edge), ensuring high stability with zero ringing.

4. COMMON-MODE REJECTION RATIO (CMRR)
   - Value: ~90 dB
   - Details: Derived from open-loop differential gain (+80 dB) and 
     common-mode gain (~-10 dB), confirming excellent common-mode 
     noise suppression.

5. POWER SUPPLY REJECTION RATIO (PSRR+)
   - Value: ~80 dB
   - Details: Calculated from differential gain (80 dB) and low-frequency 
     positive supply gain (0 dB from VDD AC injection), indicating solid 
     ripple rejection at low frequencies.

6. CLOSED-LOOP UNITY-GAIN RESPONSE
   - Status: Flat 0 dB response
   - Details: Verified with the output tied to the inverting input and 
     input common-mode matched to the DC operating point (1.60 V), 
     showing a completely flat response up to the cutoff frequency.
